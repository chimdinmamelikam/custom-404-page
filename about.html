About Me

Hi, I’m Chimdinma Melikam — a techie with a builder’s heart and a passionate Frontend Developer in training. My journey into tech began long before I wrote my first line of code. I have always loved creating solutions and helping people work smarter.

I started out building WordPress websites for clients, which opened my eyes to the world of web creation. But in 2025, I took a bold step and joined HerTechTrail Academy (Cohort 14) to dive deeper into frontend development. Since then, I’ve been learning HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap — building real projects, taking challenges, and growing consistently.

What I love about frontend development is how it blends creativity and logic. Every day, I’m turning blank screens into beautiful, responsive designs that people can actually use. Whether it’s designing interfaces, testing ideas, or improving user experience — building excites me.

Beyond coding, I enjoy researching, learning new tools, and creating digital solutions. My tech journey is still unfolding, and I’m excited about the developer I am becoming.

Welcome to my world — where curiosity meets code, and where I’m building my future one project at a time. 💛
